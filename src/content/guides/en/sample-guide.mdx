<TLDR>State your goal, add only the context the task needs, and describe the format you want. Review the response before relying on it.</TLDR>

## Define the Task

<Steps>
<Step>Choose a small writing task, such as making a packing checklist.</Step>
<Step>Describe the audience and the format you would find useful.</Step>
<Step>Remove names, addresses, and anything the task does not need.</Step>
</Steps>

<Prompt label="A Packing Checklist">Create a packing checklist for a short train trip. Group items by purpose. Use short bullet points. Ask about missing context instead of making assumptions.</Prompt>

<Tip>A narrow task makes it easier to judge whether the answer helps.</Tip>

## Review the Response

Read each suggestion and remove anything that does not fit your situation.

<Warning>A fluent answer can still contain a mistake. Check details that affect your plans.</Warning>

<Safety>Keep personal information out of the prompt. Use a placeholder when context is necessary.</Safety>
